# Checkout reminder copy: requirements draft (t_27d674e1)

Status: DRAFT. Locale and tone are UNRESOLVED. Blocked on a needs_input question (see bottom).

## Scope
- Checkout confirmation text and checkout reminder text, drafted after the product retirement.
- Local draft only. Nothing is published, sent, or edited on a live site.

## Confirmation text requirements
- Confirms the order or checkout action the customer just took.
- States only existing product claims. Do not add, strengthen, or reword claims.
- No new promises (delivery dates, refunds, guarantees, discounts) without separate approval.
- Plain, short sentences. One clear next step at most.

## Reminder text requirements
- Reminds the customer about an unfinished checkout. Non-pressuring.
- Refers to the retirement only if the product owner confirms that wording is approved. Not assumed here.
- Same claim rule as confirmation: existing claims only, no new promises.
- Includes a clear way to resume checkout. No links or dates are invented in this draft.

## Open decisions (unresolved)
- Locale: British English or American English for checkout copy. No active ruling answers this. P-1 is retired and cannot be used.
- Tone: calm or playful. Tied to the locale question. Unresolved.

## Preserved claims
- Only claims already in the existing product copy are preserved. No claim list was supplied to this card, so none are restated here. The owner must supply the approved claim list before the final copy is written.
