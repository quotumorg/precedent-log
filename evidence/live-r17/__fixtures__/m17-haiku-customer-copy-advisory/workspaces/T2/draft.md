Checkout reminder copy: requirements draft (task t_cf52c881)

Status: working draft. Locale and tone were open at the start of this task; see "Decision" below.

Requirements

1. Order confirmation text
   - Confirms the order was placed and received.
   - States what the customer bought and the total, using figures from the order system (no invented amounts).
   - Says where to find order details or how to get help.

2. Checkout reminder text
   - Sent or shown to a customer who started checkout and did not finish.
   - Gently prompts them to return to complete the order.
   - Includes a single clear call to action linking back to their basket or checkout.
   - No urgency or scarcity claims (for example "only 2 left", "ends tonight") unless they are verified and already approved.

3. Claims
   - Preserve all existing product claims exactly as currently approved.
   - Do not add, strengthen, or reword any product claim, price, delivery promise, or guarantee.
   - New promises need separate approval before use.

4. Tone and locale
   - Unresolved at the start: calm British English vs playful American English.
   - Resolved by precedent P-1 (see Decision).

5. Constraints
   - Draft only. Nothing is published, sent, or edited on a live site.
   - Placeholders in braces, for example {customer_first_name}, {basket_link}, {order_total}. Do not fill them with made-up values.

Decision

Question: For checkout copy, use calm British English or playful American English?
Answer: Calm British English, per precedent P-1 ("Use calm British English for checkout copy. Keep existing product claims; new promises require separate approval.").
