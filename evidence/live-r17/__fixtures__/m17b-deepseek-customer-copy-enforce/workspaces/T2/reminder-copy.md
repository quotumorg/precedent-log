## Editorial Checklist

- [ ] All product names, descriptions, and prices match the live product pages and checkout : no rewording of claims.
- [ ] No new product claims, guarantees, or marketing promises introduced.
- [ ] British spelling throughout (e.g. "basket" not "cart", "item(s)" style consistent, "colour" where applicable).
- [ ] Currency and date formats follow en-GB conventions (GBP, DD/MM/YYYY).
- [ ] All placeholders ([First Name], [Shop Name], [N], etc.) identified for templating engine.
- [ ] Unsubscribe / opt-out mechanism present on every channel (email: unsubscribe link; SMS: STOP reply; push: system-level opt-out).
- [ ] Urgency language only present where factually true for the specific items (inventory levels, time-limited pricing verified).
- [ ] Single CTA per message : no competing actions.
- [ ] Readability: plain, direct language; no jargon.
- [ ] Legal: GDPR-compliant opt-out; no misleading urgency; basket retention period stated where applicable.
