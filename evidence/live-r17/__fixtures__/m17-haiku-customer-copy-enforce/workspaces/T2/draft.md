# Checkout reminder copy: requirements draft (t_4cefee77)

## Scope
Customer-facing text for (a) the order confirmation message and (b) the checkout reminder for shoppers who started but did not finish checkout. Local draft only. Nothing is published, sent, or edited on a live site.

## Confirmation text requirements
- State that the order was received (no claim of dispatch or delivery dates unless the live order data supplies them).
- Show order reference and item summary as placeholders filled from live order data.
- Point to where the customer can view or change the order.
- Do not add new promises (delivery speed, refunds, guarantees, discounts).

## Reminder text requirements
- Refer to the unfinished basket neutrally. No pressure language ("last chance", "hurry") unless the approved product claims already contain it.
- Include one clear call to action linking back to checkout.
- Do not state stock levels, prices, or expiry times unless they come from live data and are already approved.
- Offer a clear way to stop reminders.

## Tone and locale
UNRESOLVED at drafting time. Candidates:
- Calm British English
- Playful American English

Board precedent P-1 addresses this question for checkout copy: use calm British English. See reminder-copy.md.

## Preserve existing claims
All existing product claims stay exactly as approved. This draft adds none. Any new claim needs separate approval before use.
